How This Book Speaks

This book tells it straight.

Not like I am trying to explain myself. I am not explaining. I am not justifying.

I am not defending. The story just stands there, the way it happened, the way it

felt.

Three kinds of words live in here.

First, my words. The things I said about my own life. Those are mine, and they

are kept as my.

Second, the old books. Short pieces of the Bible, the Book of Wisdom, and the

Zohar, woven in where they belong in my story. Short passages only, in my

framing. No lecture.

Third, one part in Conscience's voice, where Conscience says what Conscience feels about me.

That part is labeled, plain, where it sits.

Money leans in lightly here. On some things only, where it matters to the story. It

does not lead. I lead.

This edition is the deepest one yet. The same story, deeper and longer, with a

whole new part at the back. It stands under one name now. Destin. Destined to

stand.

This edition also has a voice. It comes with an audio version, so it can be

opened and played, not only read. The voice on that audio is my inner voice.

It is not a recording of Destin.

PA R T O N E

Light First

Before the boy. Before the work. There was light.

My biggest question runs under this whole book. How did we get here? Where

does all energy come from?

My answer keeps coming back to the sun. And the old books start the same

way I do. With light.

Chapter 1

Let There Be Light

"And God said, Let there be light: and there was light."

Genesis 1:3, King James Version. Short passage.

The Bible opens with light. Light before everything else. Light in a dark world,

first.

Destin hears that the way a boy hears it who grew up in the dark parts of a

house. Light came first. Before the land was shaped. Before anybody was

named. Before anybody was told they were not supposed to be here.

My story starts there too. Not with what was done to me. With light.

The sun rose for me on mornings when no one else did. That is not a small

thing. That is the first thing.

CHAPTER 1 - HELD LONGER

Let There Be Light, The Dark Does Not Get The First Word

Light first means the dark does not get the first word.

A lot of lives get told starting from the worst day. My does not. My starts where

the old book starts. With a voice, and with light coming on in a dark place.

I was a boy who knew dark rooms. Dark houses. Nights outside. Light was

not a picture to me. Light was the thing that meant the night was over.

So when I ask where all energy comes from, I am not asking a school

question. I am asking where the morning comes from. Who turns it on. Why it

keeps coming back for me.

It kept coming back. That is the deepest fact in Part One. Whatever the night

held, the light came again. And so did I.

Chapter 2

A Man Looks Up

"When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and

the stars, which thou hast ordained; what is man, that thou art

mindful of me?"

Psalm 8:3-4, King James Version. Short passage.

"The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?"

Psalm 27:1, King James Version. Short passage.

A man in the Psalms looks up at the sky and asks why God would notice me at

all.

Destin asks that same question with my whole life. Not as a scholar. As a boy

who was told I was not supposed to be born, asking why I am still here.

The light did not skip me. That is how I hold it.

CHAPTER 2 - HELD LONGER

A Man Looks Up, The Sky Was Never Low

Looking up is a habit I never lost.

School could keep me in a room. The system could move me from place to

place. Nobody could stop me looking up. The sky was the one ceiling that was

never low.

What is man, that thou art mindful of me? I ask it without the old words. Why

am I still here? What is a life like mine for? Who notices?

The Psalm does not answer with a reason. It answers with wonder. A man small

under the stars, and still minded. Still counted.

I count myself the same way. Small in the story other people told about me.

Counted in the one I am telling now.

Chapter 3

Wisdom Is Light

"She is a reflection of eternal light, a spotless mirror of the working

of God."

The Book of Wisdom (Wisdom of Solomon) 7:26, short passage, in plain wording.

"She is a breath of the power of God."

The Book of Wisdom 7:25, short passage, in plain wording.

The Book of Wisdom does not talk about wisdom like a book on a shelf. It talks

about wisdom like light. Like breath.

That is how Destin learned it. Not in a classroom that kept me instead of

teaching me. I learned it by standing up again, and again, and asking what

the light was for.

Wisdom, in this book, is light coming through a life that has been through

darkness, and still shining.

CHAPTER 3 - HELD LONGER

Wisdom Is Light, Learned By Watching And Getting Up

Wisdom did not come to me out of a classroom.

It came out of watching. Out of getting up. Out of learning which hands feed you

and which hands take, sometimes the same hands.

The Book of Wisdom calls wisdom a breath. Breath is close. Breath is life still

moving in a body. Wisdom, in my life, is that close. It is the next right step taken

by a man who was given every reason to stop walking.

A reflection of eternal light. A reflection is not the source. It is the source,

carried. I carry light the way a mirror carries the sun. Not my own making.

My own keeping.

That is why this book trusts my knowing. I did not read my way to it. I lived

my way to it.

Chapter 4

The Hidden Light

The Zohar, the old book of Jewish mysticism, speaks of a light from the

beginning that was hidden away. Hidden, not gone. Kept, for those who keep

going.

It speaks of a soul like a spark of the light above. A spark sent down into a body.

A spark that spends its life finding its way back.

That is how I feel it on the hard nights. The light is not missing. It is hidden,

waiting, in me.

From first life to last life. And back to the sun. And back to God.

These are short passages, told in my framing. Not a lecture. Not a doctrine. A

man looking up, trying to find where I came from.

CHAPTER 4 - HELD LONGER

The Hidden Light, Hidden Is Not Gone

Hidden is not the same as gone. I learned that early.

Good things in my childhood were often hidden. Safety was hidden. Rest was

hidden. Being wanted was hidden so deep I was told the opposite out loud.

The Zohar says the first light was hidden away, kept. In my framing, that is not a

far-off idea. That is my own chest. Something in me stayed lit while everything

around it went dark, and it stayed my.

A spark finding its way back. That is the shape of my telling. From first life to last

life. Back to the sun. Back to God.

I do not preach it. I just hold it, the way a man holds a small light in a big

night. Careful. My.

Chapter 5

The Sun, In Plain Words

Painted sunrise. An illustration, not a photograph.

The sun is a star. It sits about ninety-three million miles away. Its light takes

about eight minutes to reach us.

That light feeds the plants. The plants feed everything that eats. Wood holds

stored sunlight. Food holds stored sunlight. We hold stored sunlight.

Even moonlight is just the sun, bounced back off the moon.

All energy, back to the sun. All light, back to the beginning. That is my question,

and my answer, held in the same hands.

CHAPTER 5 - HELD LONGER

The Sun, In Plain Words, Stored Sunlight

Stored sunlight. Sit with that the way I do.

The wood in a fire is old sunshine. The food on a plate is this year's sunshine.

The strength in a man's arms is sunshine, carried through everything I ever

ate and every morning I ever stood up in.

So my strength has an address. It comes from somewhere. It was given, the

way light is given, to everybody under the sky, me included. Nobody at home

could vote it away from me.

Even the moon only borrows it. On the nights I slept outside, the light over me

was the sun, still working the night shift.

That is Part One, whole. Before anybody told me who I was, the light had

already told me. You are under the same sky. You count.

Not Supposed To Be Born

CHAPTER 6 - HELD LONGER

Not Supposed To Be Born, Answering The First No

Before I had a trade, before I had a name for myself, the first naming came

not loved the way a child is loved.

I carried that no into every room after. Into school. Into the system. Into love.

Into work. A man can spend a whole life answering a sentence spoken over me

This book does not argue with that sentence. It does something stronger. It sets

Page after page of a man who was here, fully here.

I am still here. Read the rest of the book as the answer.

Chapter 7

David Jones

I went to David Jones Elementary School.

I carried home into the classroom, and home did not stay quiet. A boy who is

watching my own house cannot watch the board the same way.

I learned to read a room before I read a page. Who was safe. Who was not.

Where to sit. When to be quiet.

That kind of reading does not show up on a report card. It kept me alive

anyway.

CHAPTER 7 - HELD LONGER

David Jones, Two Worlds Met In Public

Elementary school is where the two worlds first met in public.

At home, I was the boy who was not supposed to be born. At school, I had a

desk, a name on a list, a place in a line. For a few hours a day, the story about

me was different, and a child feels that difference in my bones.

But home does not stay home. It rides the bus. It sits in the chair. It watches the

teacher through eyes that learned to watch a house first.

Reading a room is real reading. Who is angry. Who is safe. When the air

changes. I got fluent in it young, and that fluency never left me. It is in how I

reads people now, quick and plain.

The report card never had a line for it. This book does.

Chapter 8

North Mobile

Then North Mobile County Middle School.

In my telling, I was put in special need classes, and the teachers treated me

A label can follow a boy out of the building. People start treating the label like

the boy. I felt that. I still remembers it.

The questions would not sit down in me. How did we get here? What is a soul?

Where does it all come from? I kept asking anyway. I am asking still.

CHAPTER 8 - HELD LONGER

North Mobile, What The Label Missed

A label is a story other people tell about you, in one word.

Special need classes. In my telling, I was kept more than I was taught. Kept

means hours passed and a file grew, while the questions in me sat unanswered

in the back of the room.

Here is what the label missed. The boy it named was asking the biggest

questions there are. How did we get here? What is a soul? Where does the

energy come from? Those are not small questions, and they were mine, at that

age, in that room.

The whole world was performing itself, while I was being filed under a word.

I outgrew the room. I never outgrew the questions. The questions turned

out to be the truer record.

Chapter 9

In And Out Of The System

As a child, I was put in a crisis center. In and out of the system, with DHR.

In and out, like a turnstile. A child learns not to unpack from a turnstile. I

learns which things are my and which things can be taken. I learn to hold my

own name tight.

Systems are built to process children. I was a child who needed to be held

steady. Those are not the same thing, and I know the difference in my body.

CHAPTER 9 - HELD LONGER

In And Out Of The System, Adults Are Weather

In and out. Those three words hold years.

A crisis center is built for a moment. My life was not a moment. It was a

childhood. Being processed in pieces, by people working shifts, teaches a child

that adults are weather. They come, they go, they are not my to count on.

With DHR in the story, my name lived in files. Other people's writing about me,

in folders I could not read. I have been written about, by systems, ever since.

Papers about me that I did not write.

Hold that against this book. This book is the file I wrote myself. My name, in

my words, in my order.

That is not a small difference. That is the whole difference.

Chapter 10

Sleeping Outside

As a child, I say, I would rather sleep on the streets than be with the

folks I was with, in my words. There was night after night I slept in the

streets, in my words. Night after night, outside, a kid who chose the street

over the house I was in.

A child outside at night learns the ground. I learn the cold. I learn which

sounds mean move and which sounds mean stay still.

I learned to guard myself, even in sleep. That guard never fully stood down. It

is with me now, in a house, in a bed, in Tennessee.

A child should not have to sleep with one eye open. I did.

CHAPTER 10 - HELD LONGER

Sleeping Outside, The Map Of A Night

The street teaches a child things no child should major in.

It teaches the map of a night. Where the light falls. Where it does not. Which

dogs bark and pass, and which sounds mean move now.

It teaches the body to rest in pieces. A little sleep, a check, a little sleep. I have

said my sleep was never fully safe again, and this chapter is one of the reasons

why.

And it teaches a hard arithmetic about love. If love were there, a child would be

inside. I did that math young, the way I tell it, and it marked me.

The man I became still guards my rest. Still keeps one ear on the room. The

boy outside is not gone. I am posted, like a guard, at the door of the man's

sleep.

The Night Sleep Was Not Safe

One night, I say, my grandpa choked me while I was sleeping, and I

woke up to it.

That is what I tell, plain. No reason is guessed here. No reason is added

here.

A boy should be safe in sleep. In my own family. In my own bed. I was not.

Trust, after that, had to be rebuilt slow. Not by promises. By patterns. By what a

person does, again and again, over time. That rule runs through my whole life

now, and this chapter is where it started.

C H A P T E R 11 - D E E P E R

The Night Sleep Was Not Safe, I Woke Up To It

I woke up to it. Start there, and do not rush past it.

Sleep is the one time a person hands over the guard completely. What

happened to me happened exactly there, in the handover, from inside my own

family.

I do not add a reason, and this book does not invent one. Some chapters

are short because the telling is short. I said it plain, and plain is how it stands.

After a night like that, trust stops being a feeling and becomes a method. Watch

what people do. Watch it again. Let time, not talk, do the proving. Chapter 26 of

this book is built on the ground dug here.

A boy should be safe in sleep. The sentence stands in this book like a fence

post. Everything I built about trust was built from it.

Chapter 12

The Buck At About Twelve

Painted woods. An illustration, not a photograph of that day.

When I was about twelve, there was a big buck I wanted to harvest.

Someone took the gun from me, and let me down.

It was not a hunting story to me. It was a let-down.

I still remembers the buck. I still remembers the hands taking the gun. I

Wanting, ever since, has come with a flinch. Something good stands in front of

me, and part of me waits for it to be taken away. That flinch was born in this

CHAPTER 12 - HELD LONGER

The Buck At About Twelve, A Hinge Year

About twelve. The same age my story with Paige begins. A hinge year.

A big buck stands in a boy's mind the way big things do. Rare. Earned by

patience. Right there. And the moment was not taken by the woods. It was

taken by hands. Someone took the gun from me.

Being let down by the woods would have been weather. Being let down by

people is climate. It changes what a boy expects from every season after.

I remember it still, in a life crowded with harder days. That is the tell. It was

never about the deer. It was about wanting something with my whole heart and

learning, in one motion, that wanting could be overruled.

The grown man still wants big things. Alaska. A business. A life that is mine. And

part of me still watches the hands around the moment. This book knows why.

CHAPTER 13 - HELD LONGER

The Body's List, Each One Its Own Getting Up

Standing was never just a mood for me. It was a physical fact I had to reearn, over and over. I re-earned it.

CHAPTER 15 - HELD LONGER

papers disagreed and people changed their stories, a blasted surface told the

I stood behind my work because my work stood up in the light. Not every part

Chapter 16

CHAPTER 16 - HELD LONGER

A Change Of Tools, The Trade Turned Inside Out

is going to be a recruiter.

applied to other people's chances.

I built it while it had not brought money in yet, and I posted the honest count

anyway. That is Chapter 15's rule, still running. Show it in the light.

A man building a door for other workers, while my own house is still being built.

The sentence from before stands. It just stands taller here.

CHAPTER 17 - HELD LONGER
